## $(date +%Y-%m-%d) - Adding ARIA labels to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** In icon-heavy luxury interfaces, icon-only buttons (like share, zoom, rotate, lighting mode) often lack accessible names, making them difficult for screen reader users to understand.
**Action:** Always add `aria-label` or `title` attributes (or visually hidden text) to buttons that rely solely on icons to convey their function, ensuring they are accessible to all users.
