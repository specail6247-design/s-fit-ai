## 2024-05-18 - Minimalist Service Triggers
**Learning:** For luxury and minimalist UI designs, service essentials like help and login should follow a "hidden until needed" philosophy to keep the primary interaction space pure.
**Action:** Integrated Support Hub and Member Access triggers as low-opacity, unstyled text buttons in the top right corner of the RealLifeFitting component, relying on overlay components (modals/drawers) for the high-fidelity experiences.
