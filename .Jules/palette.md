## 2025-04-06 - Accessible Icon-Only Buttons in 3D/AR Overlays
**Learning:** Found a pattern across immersive features (`ARLiveFitting`, `LuxuryGarmentDetail`) where critical camera and UI controls are icon-only `<button>`s relying exclusively on `<span className="material-symbols-outlined">`. This makes them completely invisible to screen readers while looking like standard buttons visually, and their focus states are extremely hard to see against complex AR camera feeds or dark 3D backgrounds.
**Action:** When implementing icon-only buttons for 3D/AR overlays, ALWAYS include an explicit `aria-label` attribute on the `<button>`, add `aria-hidden="true"` to the inner `span` containing the icon font, and append high-contrast focus styles like `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none` to guarantee both screen reader and keyboard accessibility over complex imagery.
