## 2024-05-15 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon Buttons
**Learning:** A common accessibility oversight in this app is the omission of `aria-label`s on icon-only buttons (like `✕` for close, or Google Material Symbols icons). This renders them invisible or unhelpful to screen reader users.
**Action:** Proactively check and add `aria-label`s to any button or link that uses only visual cues or symbols to convey its purpose.
