## 2026-09-15 - Auth Modal Form & Social Login Accessibility
**Learning:** The `AuthButton` modal containing standard authentication inputs (email, password) and social login buttons lacked fundamental accessibility. Icon-only elements (close buttons, emojis) and unlabelled inputs were entirely opaque to screen readers, causing significant barriers for keyboard and non-visual navigation within this crucial user journey.
**Action:** When auditing custom modal implementations—especially login flows heavily reliant on visual styling—proactively inject `aria-label` for inputs lacking visible text `<label>`s, add `aria-busy` on async submit buttons, and explicitly hide decorative icons (`aria-hidden="true"`) to construct a semantic, navigable experience. Ensure these are verified during the frontend review process.
